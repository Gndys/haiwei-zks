Multi-branch vehicle owner Wiring Harness

The main Wiring Harness assembly for the vehicle's multi-channel electrical appliances has clear branch directions, and the outer layer is covered with bellows/tape, which facilitates installation and improves wear resistance and tensile strength.

Can be customized according to car model and function: plug model, wire diameter ratio, branch length, sheath material and label identification method can all be adjusted.

Multi-plug body Wiring Harness

The multi-interface combination body wiring harness can be used to connect and extend circuits such as body electrical appliances, instruments, switches and sensors, making the wiring more regular.

Supports terminal crimping, heat shrink protection, branch marking and color differentiation to facilitate assembly and later maintenance.

White plug harness assembly

The harness assembly uses white connectors, with reliable locking and clear plugging and unplugging feel, and is suitable for quick docking of local functional modules of the body/chassis.

The wire diameter, wire color and outlet direction can be selected according to needs, and sheaths, fixed buckles or waterproof structures can be added.

Waterproof two-core tail cable (red and black)

Two-core waterproof tail cable, red and black color matching to make it easy to distinguish the positive and negative poles, suitable for common 12V/24V power consumption scenarios such as fog lights, fans, and pumps.

Supports customization of wire diameter and length, and optional high-temperature resistant wires, thickened sheaths, and waterproof terminals to meet the needs of different working conditions.

Two-core tail wire (blue and yellow)

The two-core tail wire is used for power supply or signal extension. The blue and yellow color scheme facilitates installation and circuit identification, and can be flexibly matched with different plug or terminal forms.

It can be cut according to the actual wiring length, and supports the installation of detailed configurations such as corrugated pipes, braided mesh, and cable tie fixing points.

Ring terminal return line (with identification)

Ring terminal cord set suitable for grounding/circuit connection. The terminals are firmly crimped, and heat shrinkable tube markings can be added according to the circuit number to facilitate assembly and management.

Optional tin-plated terminals, different hole diameters and wire diameter specifications can meet the installation needs of different environments such as cabins and chassis.

Multi-model plug Wiring Harness collection

Covering a variety of commonly used plug and terminal specifications, it is suitable for rapid prototyping and selection comparison; it can also be processed and packaged for delivery according to the list.

Supports small batches of multi-category mixed orders, sorting by label/color/model, reducing on-site assembly error rates.

H7 ceramic lamp holder adapter cable

Ceramic lamp holders are high temperature resistant and anti-aging, and are suitable for headlight bulb replacement or modification scenarios to reduce the risk of deformation and poor contact in high temperature environments.

Different outlet methods can be made according to the car light space and installation direction, and sealing rings, anti-loosening structures and heat-resistant wires can be optionally added.

Two-core waterproof plug (yellow seal)

Two-core waterproof connector with yellow seal sealing structure, firm plug-in and pull-out, suitable for electrical connections outdoors, on chassis and in humid environments.

It can be used to produce mating tail wires, extension wires and branch wires, and the wire diameter, wire length and terminal plating can be selected as needed.
